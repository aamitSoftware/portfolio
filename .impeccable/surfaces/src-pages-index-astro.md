## Scope

Home page (`src/pages/index.astro`) plus the case-study template it links to. Mode: Experience (portfolio; the work leads).

## Audience and job

Israeli SaaS hiring managers, recruiters, engineers. Job: decide in one screen that Amit ships production systems, then open a case study or email him. Primary action: Email me. Secondary: open a project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a press sheet straight off a Landa press, not a website wearing a print sticker. It refuses the category default of hero headline + stat row + project card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool coated stock on a press-bed backdrop. Sheet anatomy carries the page: trim crop marks, registration targets at edge midpoints, a slug line holding the nav, a colour-control strip of real C/M/Y/K patches and overprints along the foot. Process inks only in plates, marks and status; everything else is key black on paper. Archivo variable throughout, width axis as voice. Print mode multiplies (subtractive), Screen mode adds light (dark theme).

STORY: Visitor reads the name, one plain sentence of what he builds, sees five honest job rows with status, opens one, or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slug nav top edge. "Amit / Asher" stacked, left-aligned, ~8.5rem at desktop, three ink plates drifting into register. One sentence and role line below, Email me button. First work rows peek above the fold on desktop.

FORM: Pinned by the user ("Press proof", chosen in planning); position 1 of my list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: Print/Screen toggle re-mixes the plates between subtractive and additive. Motion: one load-time register; View Transitions on row title to case-study heading.
