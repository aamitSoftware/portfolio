## Problem

A web application firewall throws thousands of alerts. Someone has to work out which ones are a real attack, write a rule that blocks it without blocking real users, and prove the rule works. That person is usually overloaded.

## The plan

RuleSmith acts as a junior analyst that never ships anything on its own.

<Flow
  label="How RuleSmith handles an incident"
  steps={[
    'Score: rate ModSecurity traffic and group it into incidents',
    'Triage: LangGraph agents investigate with RAG over CRS, CAPEC and CWE',
    'Draft: propose a SecRule for the incident',
    'Red-team: another agent tries to bypass the rule',
    'Prove: replay attack and normal traffic through a sandbox WAF',
    'Approve: a human reviews the evidence and decides',
  ]}
/>

## Why it's interesting

The hard part isn't generating a rule. It's evidence: the rule has to block the attack, let normal traffic through, and survive a deliberate bypass attempt before anyone sees it.

## Where it stands

Planned. It pairs with AgentGate: both are about letting AI do real work while a person stays in control of what actually changes.
